# Hey, I'm Han 👋

I'm the co-founder and CEO of [Mintlify](https://mintlify.com) — a documentation platform used by 20,000+ developer teams around the world. I started Mintlify during my senior year at Cornell, went through Y Combinator's W22 batch, and have been building it full-time ever since. Before that, I interned at Meta, worked as a staff engineer at Bettermode, and spent time on the investment team at Bain Capital Ventures.

I care deeply about developer experience, the future of AI agents, and making technical knowledge more accessible — both for humans and for machines. This site is a small corner of the internet where I share a bit about my work, my background, and the things I'm thinking about.

## What I'm Building

Mintlify started with a simple observation: developer documentation is almost always an afterthought, and it shows. Docs are hard to write, hard to maintain, and even harder to make beautiful. We set out to fix that — and what began as a better way to write docs has grown into something much bigger.

Today, Mintlify is the documentation infrastructure for some of the most important companies in tech, including Anthropic, Microsoft, and Amazon. We're building the platform where developers publish their knowledge, and increasingly, where AI agents come to understand and interact with products. The line between "docs for humans" and "docs for agents" is blurring fast — and we think that's one of the most interesting problems in software right now.
