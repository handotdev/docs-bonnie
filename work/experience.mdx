Han's career is a thread of bets made early and often — on farmers needing better tech, on startup communities deserving better tools, on developers deserving documentation that doesn't feel like an afterthought. From a summer internship writing code in Vancouver to building one of Y Combinator's most-loved developer tools, each chapter has been defined by a bias toward shipping and a genuine curiosity about what comes next.

## Timeline

<Steps>
  <Step title="Software Engineer Intern — VanHack (2018)">
    **Vancouver, BC · Summer 2018**

    Han's first professional engineering role, working at VanHack — a platform connecting international tech talent with companies in North America and Europe. An early taste of product-minded engineering and the global nature of the software industry.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Co-Founder — FoodFul (2019–2020)">
    **2019 · Raised $150K**

    Han co-founded FoodFul, a cloud-based monitoring system for dairy cows that used sensor data to track animal health and farm productivity. The company raised $150K in funding and gave Han his first real lesson in building hardware-meets-software products and talking to customers whose world looks nothing like Silicon Valley.
  </Step>

  <Step title="SVB Fellow (January 2020)">
    **Silicon Valley Bank · Jan 2020**

    Selected as an SVB Fellow, a program recognizing promising early-stage founders and giving them access to the broader startup ecosystem. A signal that FoodFul's ambitions were being noticed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Software Engineering Intern — Facebook / Meta (Summer 2020)">
    **Greater Seattle Area · Jun–Aug 2020**

    Spent a summer at Facebook engineering at scale. In Han's own words: *"Moved fast and broke things."* Working inside one of the world's largest engineering organizations meant absorbing how high-velocity teams think about systems, reliability, and developer experience — lessons that would later shape Mintlify's product philosophy.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Co-Founder — pe•ple (Oct 2020–Feb 2021)">
    **Oct 2020–Feb 2021**

    Han co-founded pe•ple, a community platform purpose-built for early-stage startups. The product helped founders and their teams create spaces for collaboration, discussion, and momentum-building — the kind of community infrastructure that startup ecosystems thrive on but rarely build well.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Acquisition by Bettermode — Staff Software Engineer (Feb–May 2021)">
    **Bettermode · Feb–May 2021**

    pe•ple was acquired by Bettermode in February 2021. Han stayed on as a Staff Software Engineer, applying everything learned from the pe•ple product to help Bettermode build communities for larger, growth-stage startups. A short but formative stint that closed the loop on his first acquired company.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Investor — Bain Capital Ventures (Summer 2021)">
    **Jun–Aug 2021**

    Han joined Bain Capital Ventures as an investor. By his own account, he *"drank coffee"* — a self-deprecating nod to the very human, relationship-driven work of early-stage venture. Sitting on the other side of the table sharpened his instincts for what makes a compelling product and a fundable team.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Co-Founder & CEO — Mintlify (Oct 2022–Present)">
    **San Francisco, CA · Oct 2022–Present**

    Han co-founded Mintlify as part of Y Combinator's W22 cohort. What started as a better way to write and host documentation has grown into the documentation platform trusted by 20,000+ teams — from fast-moving startups to publicly traded companies. Mintlify has raised $66.8M across four funding rounds and grown to a team of 60–70 people.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Today, Mintlify powers documentation for more than **20,000 teams** worldwide. With **$66.8M in total funding** and a team of 60–70 people headquartered in San Francisco, it's the documentation platform of choice for developer-facing products that care about quality, speed, and experience.
</Info>

## Education

**Cornell University** — Ithaca, New York
Bachelor of Science in Information Science · Class of 2022 · 2018–2022

Information Science at Cornell sits at the intersection of human behavior, data, and technology — a fitting foundation for someone who would go on to build products that make technical information more human and more useful. Han's time at Cornell overlapped with the founding of FoodFul, the internship at Meta, and the early pe•ple days, making it one of the more eventful undergraduate careers in the class of 2022.
